A verifiable native Layer‑1, built one test at a time.

QuantumZXB is the selected public working identity for AI-Native L1. The proposed native-asset ticker is QZXB, but it is not deployed, listed or trademark-cleared. The project explores deterministic state transitions, authenticated peer transport, durable recovery, fixed-supply policy candidates and post-quantum signature experiments.
