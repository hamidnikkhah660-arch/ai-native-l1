Contribution guide

How to propose financial support or paid work

Send a written proposal to hamidnikkhah660@gmail.com or open the public sponsors-and-contributors discussion. A useful proposal includes:

  1. Your name or organisation and relevant technical or funding experience.
  2. The exact deliverables (for example an independent review, external seed hosting, a reproducible release pipeline or a wallet integration).
  3. Budget amount and currency, milestones, payment schedule, expected start/end dates and acceptance tests.
  4. Who reviews/accepts each deliverable, the evidence to be published, and any requested intellectual-property, licensing, confidentiality or token terms.

Do not send recovery phrases, private keys or credentials. Do not transfer money or tokens to an intermediary based only on a social message. Confirm any agreement, payment destination, invoice and legal/tax terms directly with the founder in writing.
