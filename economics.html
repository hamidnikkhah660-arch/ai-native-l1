QZXB Supply Policy: CP-0651 Candidate Economics

The active candidate policy records a 7,000,000,000 QZXB maximum accounting ceiling: 1,000,000,000 QZXB as the owner Genesis allocation and six additional 1B non-circulating accounting labels. The labels are not Genesis balances, circulating supply, transfer balances or automatic mint authority. Block subsidy is zero; block producers are compensated only by canonical transaction fees. This is a candidate policy, not an activated production Genesis or live market asset.

What CP-0651 supersedes

CP-0651 supersedes the older CP-0437 proposal of 10B QZXB allocated entirely to one owner account and the historical CP-0604 mining-allocation model. Earlier 10B/3B-mining and halving simulations remain historical evidence; they are not the active monetary policy and must not be presented as current issuance rules.

Why candidate allocation is not spendable supply

A public account identifier in a candidate manifest is not itself an issued balance. The current manifest still marks the Genesis snapshot, Q6GD descriptor, Q6CP consensus profile, Q6PN network profile and bootstrap peer set as missing; it also keeps production authorization false. The final Genesis package must be frozen, verified and signed by the owner-controlled signer before value-bearing issuance can be claimed.

No public QZXB market price, public native QZXB/USDT pool, exchange listing or liquid reserve is claimed. USDT cannot be created on QRun merely by copying the ticker. A real market requires actual counterparties, genuine quote assets, verifiable reserves and a supported settlement route.

Funding and sponsorship are separate

QZXB allocations are not cash, a signed sponsorship agreement or a guaranteed reward. Current practical funding options are voluntary open-source donations (if the external campaign remains active), grant applications, in-kind support, and written milestone-based engineering contracts. A funding proposal should state deliverables, budget and currency, milestones, timetable, acceptance criteria and payment terms. No sponsor or revenue is claimed as secured.
