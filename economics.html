QZXB Tokenomics: The 10 Billion Eight-Bucket Policy

The owner-confirmed CP-0566D direction sets one maximum gross issuance of 10,000,000,000 native QZXB: 1B (10%) to the owner at Genesis, up to 3B (30%) earned through proof-of-work mining/security, and six conditional reserves of 1B (10%) each. This is the selected economic target, not a claim that Mainnet coins are already live or spendable.

The six 1B reserves are issuance ceilings, not balances automatically credited at Genesis. Unused category allocations remain unissued. Gross issuance, circulating supply, restricted balances and actual burns must be disclosed separately.

Historical policy boundary

CP-0437 (28 October 2026) recorded a later alternative that placed all 10B QZXB with one owner at Genesis and set zero future minting/subsidy. The owner-confirmed CP-0566D direction dated 6 November 2026 now supersedes that alternative for future QRun production design. CP-0437 and its associated 10B single-owner candidate are retained as historical records; old commits and snapshots are not rewritten.

The CP-0528 QFP6 code contains candidate accounting for the selected eight-bucket model, but it is not yet frozen production consensus. The inherited research mining schedule currently emits 2,999,999,999.139264 QZXB across its integer-halving arithmetic, leaving 0.860736 QZXB of the 3B ceiling unissued. This must be documented as permanent rounding dust or corrected with independent vectors before consensus is frozen.

The 10B cap is not a price guarantee. A hypothetical $1 per QZXB implies a $10B fully diluted valuation; reaching any market price depends on actual demand, usage, liquidity, confidence and counterparties. No price, listing, revenue or investment return is promised.

Primary records: CP-0566D selected owner policy, CP-0437 historical direction, and implementation gates.

Why this does not mean the coins are live

The QFP6 Genesis/accounting path and wallet tools are candidate code, not public issuance. The production profile, account-bound Genesis commitment, reward schedule, signed category-release rules, complete supply invariants, independent nodes and recovery path still need end-to-end qualification.

No public QZXB market price, native QZXB/USDT pool, exchange listing or liquid reserve is claimed. USDT is not created on QRun by copying its ticker. A real market requires actual counterparties, genuine quote assets, verifiable reserves and a supported settlement route.

Funding and sponsorship are separate

QZXB allocations are not cash, a signed sponsorship agreement or a guaranteed reward. Current practical funding options are voluntary open-source donations (if the external campaign remains active), grant applications, in-kind support, and written milestone-based engineering contracts. A funding proposal should state deliverables, budget and currency, milestones, timetable, acceptance criteria and payment terms. No sponsor or revenue is claimed as secured.

Primary records: CP-0437 current direction, CP-0465 release decision, and v0.4 historical allocation proposal.

Project status: QRun is experimental public-alpha, not production Mainnet. No public native QZXB market or price is claimed.
