Strategic differentiator: Verifiable AI-Native Blockchain Infrastructure

The canonical QRun master roadmap maps the project across ten capability layers: core network, security and PQ transition, privacy and verifiable disclosure, performance and reliability, developer programmability, QZXB monetary rules, payments, DeFi and real-world assets, AI-agent authorization, and ecosystem operations. This is a coverage map, not a claim that every layer is implemented or production-ready.

Layer 9 priority: bounded AI-agent payment authorization

The intended differentiator is an owner-authorized, signed and auditable capability that limits an AI agent by budget, asset/network, fees, recipients or services, validity window, nonce/replay protection and revocation, with a verifiable settlement receipt. An agent must not receive unrestricted wallet access.

Status: designed / not shipped. CP-0567 records provisional policy and source-path analysis. Canonical ledger registration and revocation, atomic aggregate spend enforcement, reorg-safe persistence, wallet/SDK integration, adversarial qualification and independent review remain required before this capability can be advertised as implemented or enabled for value-bearing use.

What still blocks production Mainnet?

  1. Freeze one production Network ID, profile ID, Genesis descriptor and CP-0566D policy fingerprint; enforce 1B owner Genesis, up to 3B PoW issuance and six conditional 1B category ceilings in producer, importer, fork-choice, persistence, snapshots, replay and recovery.
  2. Resolve the QFP6 candidate mining-schedule rounding remainder: the current research arithmetic leaves 0.860736 QZXB of the 3B ceiling unissued. Document this as permanently unissued dust or adjust the schedule and independent vectors to reach the intended amount exactly without exceeding the cap.
  3. Complete the project independent-operator qualification target: demonstrate operation across unrelated hosts/providers, external inbound P2P and sustained state-root convergence. CP-0465 records three operators as an operational target for broad release qualification; this is not a protocol-level minimum for permissionless participation.
  4. Integrate full-history replay or a fully qualified checkpoint rollover with an independent monotonic anti-rollback trust domain end to end.
  5. Qualify native wallet signing/restore, transaction inclusion/confirmation, independent balance queries, a read-only public explorer/API, monitoring, reproducible release hashes and incident/reorg procedures.
  6. Complete independent protocol, consensus, cryptography and economic-security review and remediate material findings before a production decision.

Network safety and market status

The candidate listener configuration and host firewall rules do not prove router/NAT/ISP reachability. RPC and transaction ingress must remain loopback-only unless placed behind a separately hardened, authenticated and rate-limited gateway. Do not expose local administrative endpoints.

No public native QZXB price, live QZXB/USDT pool, verified market reserve or exchange listing is claimed. The separate QX contract on Ethereum Sepolia is a test asset, not native QZXB.

Primary evidence: repository audit, Mainnet/trading no-go, and 10B release gate.

Project status: QRun is experimental public-alpha, not production Mainnet. No public native QZXB market or price is claimed.
