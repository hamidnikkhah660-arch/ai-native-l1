# QRun Brand Identity — Candidate 1

- Document ID: QRUN-BRAND-001
- Status: Design candidate; not yet approved as the final production identity
- Date: 2026-11-06
- Brand: **QRun Blockchain**
- Native asset: **QZXB**
- Internal operations workspace: **QRun Mission Control**
- Positioning line: **Verifiable AI-Native Blockchain Infrastructure**

## Brand architecture

Use **QRun Blockchain** as the independent public product and ecosystem name. Use **QRun Mission Control** for the internal project-health, release, evidence, and risk dashboard. Attribute the project to **Hamid Nikkhah, Founder** in the founder/about area; do not make the founder's personal name the product or network brand. Keep QZXB as the ticker, not a competing product name.

## Candidate visual system

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Space Navy | `#07152F` | Primary dark backgrounds |
| Midnight Blue | `#0B2348` | Secondary surfaces |
| Electric Blue | `#2563EB` | Primary brand/action color |
| Signal Cyan | `#22D3EE` | Highlights, links, active states |
| Ice Silver | `#C7D5E8` | Wordmark, borders, neutral emphasis |
| Cloud White | `#F4F8FF` | Light surfaces and readable text |
| Graphite | `#17243A` | Light-theme text and dark neutral surfaces |

Use blue/cyan gradients sparingly for hero artwork and the symbol. Functional text, charts, and status labels must retain strong contrast and must not rely on glow effects. Provide light and dark themes; never encode pass/fail or risk solely by hue.

## Symbol and geometry

The candidate symbol combines a strong circular **Q** (continuity and verifiable ledger history), a forward orbital stroke (network connectivity and progress), and small node/cube motifs (independent nodes and composable infrastructure). Prefer clean geometry, balanced negative space, and legibility at 16–24 px. Avoid excessive 3D bevels, tiny circuit details, gradients that disappear in print, or visual claims of quantum security.

## Typography and layout

Use a modern neutral sans-serif such as Inter, with system sans-serif fallbacks. Keep the wordmark spelling exactly `QRun`; use `BLOCKCHAIN` as a secondary descriptor. Use a consistent spacing scale, restrained rounded corners, thin network-line motifs, and simple geometric iconography. The Mission Control interface should prioritize status, evidence links, blockers, owners, and next actions over decorative charts.

## Asset variants required before final approval

1. Symbol-only SVG, monochrome and full-color.
2. Horizontal wordmark SVG for dark and light backgrounds.
3. Square app/favicon icon, tested at 16, 32, 48, and 128 px.
4. Print-safe one-color version.
5. Accessible UI color tokens and examples for buttons, links, status, charts, and focus indicators.
6. Clear-space, minimum-size, and incorrect-use guidance.

The companion SVG files in this directory are geometric candidates, not trademark-cleared final assets. Check domain, trademark, and marketplace conflicts before commercial launch.

## Evidence and marketing guardrails

Brand design does not establish protocol capability. Do not use `quantum-secure`, `fully post-quantum`, `production-ready`, `mainnet-ready`, `audited`, `trustless`, `low-fee`, or performance superlatives as factual claims without corresponding current evidence. Describe the PQ transition with precise component-level status. The positioning line is an identity statement, not a security certification.

## Approval gate

This candidate should be reviewed at desktop, mobile, favicon, monochrome, light-theme, and dark-theme sizes. Final approval requires the project owner to select or revise the direction. No site deployment or existing logo replacement is authorized by this document.
