**Policy ID:** CP-0651-A  
**Date:** 2026-11-27  
**Status:** Owner-authorized policy draft for candidate economics; not a production Genesis, issuance transaction, vesting contract, legal opinion, or authorization to transfer value.  
**Parent policy:** CP-0651 — 7,000,000,000 QZXB maximum accounting ceiling.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This appendix assigns explicit purposes and conditional release rules to the six 1,000,000,000 QZXB accounting labels under CP-0651. It preserves the 7B gross accounting ceiling, the 1B owner Genesis allocation, zero block subsidy, and canonical-transaction-fee-only producer compensation.

This policy does not create tokens, balances, entitlements, cash, liquidity, sponsor commitments, listing commitments, or mint authority. All allocations remain non-circulating accounting ceilings until the canonical monetary profile, Genesis, release controls, and authorization are finalized and independently checked.

## 3. General release controls

Every award or release must pass all applicable controls:

1. **No automatic issuance:** an accounting label is not an issued balance, transferable balance, or mint permission.
2. **Documented authority:** the relevant release mechanism must be authorized by the final candidate/production policy and must not bypass consensus or supply invariants.
3. **Purpose limitation:** tokens may be used only for the stated allocation purpose and approved agreement.
4. **Evidence before release:** recipient identity, recipient account, amount, purpose, agreement, milestone evidence, approval and transaction reference must be recorded.
5. **Staged release:** use milestone-based or time-based release; avoid one-time unrestricted releases where practical.
6. **Disclosure:** report allocated, approved, earned, released, returned/cancelled and actually burned amounts separately.
7. **No investment claims:** no allocation implies price appreciation, minimum price, profit, redemption, revenue share or investment return.
8. **Legal and tax review:** any public distribution, sale, compensation, sponsor arrangement or exchange-related payment requires jurisdiction-specific legal, tax and compliance review before execution.
9. **Unused amounts:** remain locked/non-circulating. They do not automatically migrate to another bucket.
10. **Change control:** changes require a versioned amendment, arithmetic validation, public documentation update and release review before activation.

## 4. Allocation-specific release rules

### A. Founder / Owner Genesis Allocation — 1B

Purpose: founder ownership allocation and documented support for continued QRun development.

Proposed release discipline: 48-month total vesting from the authorized Genesis date, with a 12-month cliff. At month 12, 25% becomes vested; the remaining 75% vests in equal monthly portions over months 13–48. No schedule is active until encoded, tested and authorized.

Project-development use:
- Project expenses must be recorded separately from personal expenses.
- Where founder tokens are used to fund project expenses, disclose the amount, destination, purpose and supporting record.
- Token value must not be represented as cash received; only realized proceeds or contracted funds count as project financing.
- Founder allocation does not grant hidden issuance, consensus override, privileged transaction validation or unilateral cap changes.

### B. Developers and Network Security — 1B

Eligible work includes accepted protocol code, testing, security engineering, cryptographic review, documentation, developer tooling and scoped infrastructure work.

Release controls:
- written scope and acceptance criteria before work starts;
- reviewable deliverable and recorded acceptance;
- award amount and recipient account recorded;
- independent security review remains independent and cannot be replaced by token compensation;
- use milestone awards; where time-based vesting is appropriate, specify it in the individual agreement before work begins.

### C. Community and Public Distribution — 1B

Purpose: genuine community onboarding and ecosystem participation, not price promotion.

Before any distribution, publish:
- eligibility and snapshot methodology;
- anti-Sybil and duplicate-account controls;
- maximum award per eligible participant;
- claim opening/closing dates and claim procedure;
- vesting/lockup, if any;
- treatment of unclaimed or disqualified amounts.

No participant is guaranteed a token value or return. Unclaimed amounts remain locked unless a versioned policy explicitly authorizes a different treatment.

### D. Exchange and Platform Integration — 1B

Eligible counterparties may include exchanges, wallets, custody providers, explorers, RPC providers, infrastructure platforms and other ecosystem services.

Awards require:
- a verified counterparty and written agreement;
- a defined technical or operational deliverable;
- evidence of completed integration or service;
- staged compensation tied to acceptance;
- legal/compliance review where relevant.

This reserve is not a payment-for-listing promise, listing guarantee, or default listing fee. A platform independently decides whether to list or support QZXB. No artificial volume, wash trading, undisclosed market manipulation or guaranteed liquidity may be funded.

### E. Marketing and Growth Partners — 1B

Eligible work includes educational content, disclosed campaigns, community growth, public relations, brand partnerships and measurable user acquisition.

Every campaign must define scope, budget, deliverables, measurement method, schedule and disclosure requirements. Release follows accepted deliverables. Prohibit misleading price/return claims, undisclosed paid promotion and artificial trading-volume incentives.

### F. Financial and Strategic Supporters — 1B

This is a prospective, conditional reserve. No supporter is secured merely by this policy.

A release requires:
- a signed agreement;
- verified support actually received or an independently enforceable, approved milestone obligation;
- a clear valuation and description of cash, infrastructure or services;
- recipient and account verification;
- legal, tax and compliance review.

Token awards are not company equity, a debt instrument, a redemption right, revenue share or guaranteed investment return unless a separate lawful instrument explicitly provides otherwise and is independently reviewed.

### G. Protocol Burn Reserve — 1B

This label is a reserve ceiling, not a statement that tokens have already been burned.

A burn may be reported only when:
- the tokens were actually issued and are under an authorized, verifiable control path;
- the burn method is defined in the canonical policy and is irreversible;
- the transaction or protocol event is independently verifiable;
- amount, date, reason and evidence are recorded.

Cancelling an unissued accounting ceiling is not the same as burning issued tokens. If an unissued reserve is cancelled, report it as a reduced accounting ceiling, not as a token burn. Burns are not a price-support mechanism and do not guarantee appreciation or a price floor.

## 5. Public accounting vocabulary

Use these terms consistently:

- **Allocated:** assigned a policy ceiling; not necessarily issued.
- **Approved:** authorized for a defined award or release.
- **Earned / vested:** recipient has satisfied the applicable conditions.
- **Issued:** created under the authorized canonical issuance path.
- **Released / transferred:** actually moved or made spendable under the authorized rules.
- **Circulating:** publicly available under a disclosed, reproducible methodology.
- **Burned:** actually and irreversibly removed from issued supply with verifiable evidence.
- **Cancelled ceiling:** an unissued allocation amount removed from the accounting plan; not a burn.

Never combine these categories into one “released supply” figure.

- Owner Genesis allocation remains 1,000,000,000 QZXB.
