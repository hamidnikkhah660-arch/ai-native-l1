## Professional summary

Hamid Nikkhah is an AI researcher and systems builder whose documented research interests include machine learning, quantum machine learning, evolutionary and fuzzy optimization, cognitive science, pattern recognition, and post-quantum blockchain security. He is developing QuantumZXB, an independently developed native Layer-1 and programmable-ecosystem research project. The engineering programme includes Rust-based ledger and state-transition candidates, proof-of-work and difficulty-adjustment research, persistence and recovery models, ML-DSA-65 verification experiments, hybrid-identity candidates, and an isolated EVM/revm smart-contract laboratory.

The long-term product direction is an AI-native ecosystem for developer applications and verifiable AI/compute services. LLM integration, agent identity/payment workflows and verifiable compute are roadmap areas; this profile does not claim that QuantumZXB already operates a production LLM or a measured AI-inference service.
