# Founder, Sponsor and Contributor Allocation Policy - v0.3

> **Superseded by policy v0.4 (2026-10-24).** The 4B founder / 3B mining / 3B public-goods structure below is historical, not the current allocation proposal. Use [policy v0.4](FOUNDER_SPONSOR_CONTRIBUTOR_ALLOCATION_POLICY_V04_2026-10-24.md).

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: **Owner-directed economic proposal; not yet encoded in a production Genesis or transferred on-chain. No lock or vesting is authorized by this policy.**

## 1. Owner direction

The founder allocation is 4,000,000,000 native coins (40% of the proposed 10,000,000,000 coin lifetime cap). The owner expressly retains control of this allocation. There is to be no protocol-enforced lock, escrow, cliff, vesting schedule or time-based restriction on the founder allocation under this decision.

The proposal is to invite a future financial or strategic sponsor by making up to 1,000,000,000 native coins available if the sponsor actually provides the agreed support. The project does not currently have a confirmed sponsor, and this conditional allocation is not a statement that any sponsor has already been selected, paid, contracted or granted coins.

The table is a **planning framework, not a statement of minted, transferable or on-chain balances**. The sponsor and contributor allocations come from the existing 4B founder envelope; they do not increase the 10B lifetime cap and must never be double-counted. If the contributor pool is awarded at its maximum 1B and the sponsor receives the full 1B, then the founder's directly retained discretionary amount is 2B and the four allocations exactly sum to 4B. If less than 1B is awarded to contributors, the unawarded amount stays under the founder's control unless the owner later records a different decision.

The 1B sponsor amount equals 10% of the overall 10B project cap, but it is 25% of the 4B founder allocation. Do not describe it as 10% of the founder tranche.

## 3. Sponsor qualification and milestone rule

The sponsor allocation is conditional. Before any allocation is promised as an enforceable entitlement or transferred, record:

1. the sponsor's identity or legal entity as appropriate to the intended agreement;
2. the support to be provided (cash, infrastructure, engineering, research, distribution, or another specific and measurable contribution);
3. the milestones, evidence, dates or completion conditions used to establish performance;
4. the quantity and timing of any coin allocation, recipient address verification and the consequences of partial or failed delivery;
5. conflict-of-interest, public-disclosure and sign-off requirements.

A conversation, expression of interest or unverified promise is not completion of a sponsor milestone. The sponsor remains prospective until an actual agreement and its conditions exist. This document does not grant company equity, votes, revenue share, redemption rights, a guaranteed valuation or a return.

## 4. Developer, security, legal and other professional pool

The contributor pool can compensate eligible, scoped work by developers, protocol engineers, independent security reviewers, cryptography specialists, technical writers, ecosystem integrators, and legal or other professional advisers when such work is commissioned. The possible size is 1%-10% of the total coin cap, drawn only from the founder's original 4B allocation.

Awards are not automatic. Record the work scope, agreed deliverable, acceptance evidence, recipient, quantity and approval before making an award. For independent auditors or professional advisers, payment in coins does not substitute for independence, a formal engagement or a professional opinion. No legal professional has been selected or engaged by this document.

## 5. Free founder control without supply-rule ambiguity

- No time-lock or vesting is to be added for the founder's 4B allocation because of this policy.
- The founder's discretionary coins remain subject to ordinary transaction authentication and the final network's published consensus rules.
- Owner control of allocated coins is not authority to mint above the 10B cap, rewrite history, bypass transaction validation or silently modify consensus.
- The sponsor and contributor pools remain proposed designations inside the 4B envelope until an actual award decision is recorded. They are not separate additional issuance.
- All public supply disclosures must distinguish the maximum cap, Genesis/issued supply, allocated balances and circulating-supply methodology. Do not call a balance locked if it is freely transferable.
- The final Genesis/issuance integration remains a separate engineering task. This policy does not itself mint balances or change the state machine.
