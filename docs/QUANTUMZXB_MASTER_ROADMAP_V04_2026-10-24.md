## Standing launch policy — Bitcoin-inspired permissionless-first (CP-0443)

The project must distinguish **protocol-safety blockers** from **external commercial approvals**. Bitcoin's peer-to-peer design is the reference: publish a reproducible protocol and exact consensus rules; let participants run nodes and decide locally whether to join. Do not treat exchange listings, CMC/CoinGecko/TradingView support, sponsors, auditor availability, a minimum number of independent operators signing off, or an external CAS vendor as universal permission to launch. Security review remains important evidence; fix verified critical protocol defects and disclose unresolved risk rather than waiting indefinitely for an external approval. Jurisdiction-specific obligations apply to actual offers, custody, exchange operation or regulated services and must not be conflated with a universal protocol-launch permit.

The concrete hard gates remain exact production Genesis/network/profile, one canonical monetary transition and lifetime cap across production/replay/reorg/storage, real production proof-of-work and recovery, user-controlled transaction signing, wallet submission and canonical receipts, and a functioning cross-host peer-sync/block/transaction path. For recovery, prefer consensus-native history validation, replay and cumulative-work/fork-choice; an external anti-rollback provider is not a universal protocol authority. A pruned-node or checkpoint fast-sync feature may require its own separate trust/anchor policy. The current bounded candidate archive and candidate-only monetary profile are real implementation gaps; not needing a third-party permission does not make those gaps disappear.

A native QZXB Mainnet launch and a QZXB/USDT market are separate decisions. Do not wait for a USDT pool or exchange listing to publish the native protocol, but do not claim or execute a real QZXB/USDT swap until the quote asset is authentic and accessible, the QZXB side is genuine, the market has real reserves/liquidity, and a small owner-authorized swap is independently verified. Full details and the current application are in [CP-0443](CP-0443_BITCOIN_INSPIRED_PERMISSIONLESS_LAUNCH_GATE_2026-10-28.md).
