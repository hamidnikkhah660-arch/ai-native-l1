Date: 2026-10-22
Project: QuantumZXB / AI-Native L1
Purpose: record independently checkable public-launch dependencies without representing the project as legally cleared.

## Evidence checked

1. The Dutch AFM's official [MiCAR white-paper guidance](https://www.afm.nl/en/sector/cryptopartijen/toezicht/white-papers) states that requirements depend on the crypto-asset category and applicable MiCAR provisions/exceptions; it describes submission/notification of relevant white papers, publication, consistency of marketing communications with the relevant white paper, and says the Title II white paper is notified rather than approved. The AFM says white-paper notification itself is currently free of charge.
2. The AFM's official [crypto-asset service overview](https://www.afm.nl/en/sector/cryptopartijen) states that crypto-asset service providers generally need authorisation or notification by the AFM or another EU supervisory authority to provide covered services, with specific rules/exceptions for eligible authorised entities. Services can include custody, operating a trading platform, exchange, placing, order handling, advice, portfolio management and transfer services for clients.
3. Giveth's official [FAQ](https://docs.giveth.io/faq) says project creation has no platform fee, project pages point to the owner-selected Ethereum wallet, and donations go directly to the project wallet. The same page warns that Ethereum network gas and third-party exchange/service fees can still apply and does not guarantee tax deductibility. These platform-wide statements do **not** prove QuantumZXB's page is currently available, the currently configured wallet is controlled by the owner, donations have arrived, or any tax/legal treatment applies to a particular donor or recipient.
4. The project-specific [QuantumZXB Giveth page](https://giveth.io/project/quantumzxb) could not be independently opened during this check on 2026-10-22. A prior dated repository record from 2026-10-21 is not a fresh confirmation of current page status. Do not present campaign status, eligibility, wallet control or donation totals as currently verified until the owner checks the live page directly.

## Required owner / qualified-adviser checks before any public token offer

- Identify the actual issuer/contracting party, its establishment and residence, and each intended offer/marketing jurisdiction.
- Obtain case-specific advice on how the proposed native QZXB asset and its distribution fit the relevant MiCAR category, whether an offer or admission to trading is intended, and which exemptions/white-paper notification, publication, consumer-information, marketing and other obligations apply. Do not call a white paper "AFM approved" solely because it has been notified.
- Ensure public website, white paper, supply allocation, miner-reward and market-status statements match one another and match the actual software. No claims of a listing, price, liquidity, investment return, audit or launch date without evidence.
- Before the project itself provides any covered crypto-asset services to third parties (for example custody, exchange, operation of a trading platform or transfer services), determine the applicable authorisation/notification regime and do not provide a regulated service until the required status is confirmed.
- Have a qualified adviser review legal entity/contracting, sanctions and AML responsibilities, consumer-facing donation/offer language, tax/accounting treatment, IP ownership, data handling and cross-border obligations. This checklist is not legal, financial or tax advice.
