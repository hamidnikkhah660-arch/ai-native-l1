## Why QZXB is still not cleared

An exact web search for `QZXB` / `Quantum ZXB` did not surface an obvious exact cryptocurrency listing in this search pass. That is not evidence of global availability.

Material adjacent ecosystem signals were found:
- An Ethereum ERC-20 named ZxbCoin with symbol `ZXB` appears on Ethplorer: https://ethplorer.io/address/0x98949c67F7D3F9a229aC627D6227228bE7c10DD1.
- `ZBX` is used for a BNB Smart Chain token by Billion Zone Xchange: https://www.billionzonexchange.com/.
- Older `ZX`, `QX/Qx` and `QZX` use/collision signals are recorded in the prior public collision screen.

These are not a legal conclusion that the proposed name infringes anything. They are enough to avoid claiming that `QZXB` is unique, reserved, or globally safe. Before a ticker is used on an exchange, run official trademark-register, token/explorer, exchange symbol, company-name and domain checks with an authorized applicant and intended markets. No fee has been paid and no filing/permission has been issued.

## Critical asset separation

- The already-deployed Ethereum Sepolia ERC-20 still reports `Qanvoryx Launch Candidate` / `QX`; that contract is immutable and is not renamed by this document.
- That Sepolia ERC-20 is a separate testnet asset, not a native AI-Native L1 coin and not the proposed QZXB asset.
- The native L1 has no production Mainnet token/ticker in public trading at this time. Do not tell users to buy a QZXB token, do not publish a fake contract, and do not direct valuable funds to an unverified address.
- “Quantum” is only a branding word. It does not claim end-to-end post-quantum/quantum-resistant security. The research use of ML-DSA does not prove that the whole native protocol, consensus, wallets and network have that property.
