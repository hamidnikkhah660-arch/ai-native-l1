# Community Code of Conduct

We want a technical community where people can collaborate safely, critique evidence honestly, and disagree respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; critique proposals and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome contributors across backgrounds, geographies, languages and levels of experience.
- Give credit to others' work and preserve attribution/license requirements.
- Make technical claims precise, reproducible and clear about limitations.
- Protect private information. Never publish someone else's personal data, credentials or private communications without permission.
- Report harassment, threats, spam, fraudulent investment claims, manipulated market activity, and unsafe disclosure of vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, fraudulent investment solicitation, and publishing secrets or exploit details in a way that needlessly risks users are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation in response to behavior that violates this policy. Affected people may request review through a private channel on the repository's GitHub account. Maintainers should prioritize safety, proportionality and a documented record of decisions.

This conduct policy is part of the project's own community rules; it does not create a promise of real-time moderation or a guarantee of service availability.
